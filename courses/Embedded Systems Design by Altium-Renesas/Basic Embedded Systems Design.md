> [!abstract] About these notes
> Notes for the Basic Embedded Systems Design course by Altium Education and Renesas, with one note per course module.

## 0.1 Course Modules
> [!grid]
> > [!info] Module 1
> > **[[1. Introduction to Embedded Systems|Introduction to Embedded Systems]]**
> >
> > What an embedded system is, real-time operation, MCU vs MPU vs SoC, memory, I/O, the software stack and future trends.
>
> > [!tip] Module 2
> > **[[2. Microprocessors and Microcontrollers Part 1|Microprocessors and Microcontrollers, Part 1]]**
> >
> > Inside an MCU, choosing one, MCU families, and case studies: air freshener, barcode scanner, voice control, car power module.
>
> > [!example] Module 3
> > **[[3. Microprocessors and Microcontrollers Part 2|Microprocessors and Microcontrollers, Part 2]]**
> >
> > MCU history and packages, ADC and DAC architectures, timers (GPT, AGT, WDT, RTC) and GPIO.
>
> > [!note] Module 4
> > **[[4. Introduction to Communication Protocols|Introduction to Communication Protocols]]**
> >
> > Serial vs parallel, UART, SPI, I2C, CAN, USB, Ethernet, and wireless: BLE, Wi-Fi, NFC and Zigbee.
