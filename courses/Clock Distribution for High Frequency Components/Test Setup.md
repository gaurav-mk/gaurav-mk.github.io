# Architecture Note: Three Tier Cascaded Clock Tree Synchronization
>Objective: Establish deterministic latency across primary, secondary, and tertiary levels using JESD204B Subclass 1 protocol with [Texas Instruments LMX1205](https://www.ti.com/product/LMX1205) evaluation modules.

### 1. Physical Hardware Routing

#### **Level 1 (Primary):** Standalone [LMX1205 EVB](https://www.ti.com/tool/LMX1205EVM).
- _Input:_ Pristine laboratory reference clock (1280 MHz) connected to the CLKIN port.
#### **Level 2 (Secondary):** Main master chip on the [LMX1205 Multisite Evaluation Module](https://www.ti.com/tool/LMX1205_CASCADED_REF_BOARD).
- _Interconnect:_ Phase stable coaxial cables routing CLKOUT and SYSREFOUT from Level 1 directly to CLKIN and SYSREFIN of Level 2.
#### **Level 3 (Tertiary):** Two secondary chips on the [LMX1205 Multisite Evaluation Module](https://www.ti.com/tool/LMX1205_CASCADED_REF_BOARD).
- _Interconnect:_ Permanently hardwired internally from Level 2 to Level 3 on the printed circuit board.

### 2. Device Mode Configuration

>Configurations are executed via [TICS Pro Software](https://www.ti.com/tool/TICSPRO-SW).
#### **Level 1 (Primary): SYSREF Generation Mode**
- Functions as the absolute timekeeper.
- Uses internal dividers to mathematically generate SYSREF pulses (continuous 2.5 MHz) from the main clock.
#### **Level 2 and 3 (Secondary and Tertiary): SYSREF Repeater Mode**
- SYSREF generation is disabled.
- Incoming SYSREF pulses are captured, aligned with local clock edges, and forwarded. This continuous realignment guarantees deterministic latency through the cascade.

### 3. Setup and Hold Calibration Process

>Calibration is required to prevent metastability caused by unaligned analog delays.
- **The Metastability Risk:** If an incoming SYSREF voltage transition arrives at the exact picosecond of a local clock transition, setup and hold timing rules are violated. The signal may pass one full clock cycle early or late, completely destroying synchronization.
#### **Level 2 Calibration Execution:**
- Monitor signals via a high bandwidth real time oscilloscope.
- Adjust the analog fine delay registers in the Level 2 chip.
- Shift the incoming SYSREF signal forward or backward in 1 picosecond increments.
- _Target:_ Center the SYSREF edge perfectly within the clock period, safely away from transition edges.
#### **Level 3 Calibration Execution:**
- Once Level 2 is locked and stable, repeat the exact 1 picosecond analog delay calibration for both Level 3 chips.


## **Oscilloscope Measurement Protocol for Cascaded Deterministic Latency**

>_Objective: Establish physical signal alignment targets for [JESD204B](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.jedec.org) setup and hold compliance across three hierarchy levels using a high bandwidth real time oscilloscope._

### 1. Hardware Probing Strategy

Capturing exact picosecond alignment requires strict physical probing discipline to avoid artificial signal degradation.

- **Active Differential Probing:** Standard passive probes will load the transmission lines and distort the edges. Active high speed differential probes must be used to tap the signals simultaneously.
- **Channel Density:** A minimum of three analog oscilloscope channels operating simultaneously is required to visualize the clock transition relative to the incoming and outgoing synchronization pulses.

### 2. Boundary One: Level 1 versus Level 2

This boundary crosses the external coaxial cables. The flight time of the signal is completely arbitrary and subject to mechanical cable variations.

#### **Probing Configuration:**
- Channel A: Level 1 SYSREFOUT
- Channel B: Level 2 CLKOUT  
- Channel C: Level 2 SYSREFOUT  

- **The Signal Target:** The incoming Level 1 SYSREF voltage transition must land in the exact mathematical center of the Level 2 local clock period. This ensures maximum margin against metastable hardware states.
- **Execution:** The analog fine delay register inside the Level 2 silicon must be adjusted via the software interface. The oscilloscope screen will show the Channel A edge shifting horizontally. The shift is complete when the Channel A edge sits perfectly equidistant between two Channel B clock rising edges.
- **The Latency Delta:** Once the setup and hold margin is secured, the absolute time difference between the Channel A rising edge and the Channel C rising edge is recorded. This measurement is the fixed deterministic latency for the external cable boundary.

### 3. Boundary Two: Level 2 versus Level 3

This boundary exists entirely on the internal printed circuit board of the [Texas Instruments Multisite Evaluation Module](https://www.ti.com/tool/LMX1205_CASCADED_REF_BOARD). The flight time is static because the copper traces are precisely length matched.

#### **Probing Configuration:**
- Channel A: Level 2 SYSREFOUT
- Channel B: Level 3 CLKOUT
- Channel C: Level 3 SYSREFOUT

- **The Signal Target:** The objective is identical to the first boundary. The incoming Level 2 SYSREF edge must be sampled safely by the Level 3 clock, avoiding the transition zones.
- **Execution:** Because the copper trace length is fixed, the signal may already arrive perfectly aligned. The oscilloscope screen must be monitored to verify the Channel A edge lands safely within the Channel B clock period. If the edge lands too close to a clock transition, the Level 3 analog fine delay register must be adjusted to push the edge into the safe center zone.
- **The Latency Delta:** The time difference between the Channel A rising edge and the Channel C rising edge is recorded. This value will be exceptionally small. It represents only the internal silicon propagation delay of the repeater circuit plus the static static flight time of the copper trace.

### 4. System Verification

When both boundaries are fully calibrated, the entire clock tree is locked. A final verification sweep is performed by triggering the oscilloscope on the Level 1 master pulse and measuring the final arrival time at the Level 3 output. This total time delta must remain absolutely constant across infinite power cycles to satisfy the strict medical imaging requirements.
